Ceasefire, But Make It Complicated

Trump announced an "energy ceasefire" between Ukraine and Russia, and Zelensky said sure — if Moscow plays ball too. Russia, predictably, said nothing, which in diplomacy-speak is basically a very loud "no." The proposal would halt strikes on energy infrastructure, a tactic both sides have weaponized to freeze civilians and cripple economies.

What This Means: Trump is dangling partial peace deals as a pressure tactic, but a ceasefire that only one side agrees to is just... a target. Zelensky's conditional yes is shrewd: it puts the onus on Moscow while keeping Ukraine's options open.

Houthis Keep Hitting Where It Hurts

The Iran-backed Houthis struck Riyadh's airport for the second time in two days, killing one person and injuring 11. Saudi Arabia, which has been trying to project stability and attract billions in foreign investment, is watching its gleaming capital become a target. The attacks are retaliation for Saudi involvement in Yemen's ongoing conflict — a war that has never really ended, just gotten quieter in the headlines.

What This Means: As the NYT notes, this is an asymmetrical conflict Saudi Arabia has no clean answer to — you can't bomb a drone programme into submission when the launchers cost less than a business-class ticket to Riyadh. Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 economic ambitions look shakier every time a missile lands near an airport terminal.

Nobel Goes to the Woman Who Called It Genocide

Navi Pillay, the South African former UN human rights chief and current ICJ judge, has won the Nobel Peace Prize. She chaired the UN commission that concluded Israel committed genocide in Gaza — a finding that set off political earthquakes across Western capitals. The Nobel committee cited her decades of work demonstrating that international law can actually mean something.

What This Means: This is the Nobel Committee sending a message, loudly and in writing. Awarding Pillay validates the legitimacy of international legal mechanisms at a moment when powerful states are openly flouting them — and puts fresh pressure on governments that dismissed her commission's findings as political.

RFK Jr's Vaccine Experiment Gets the Green Light

A controversial trial that will withhold the standard hepatitis B vaccine dose from thousands of newborns in Guinea-Bissau has received approval to proceed. The study, championed by HHS Secretary RFK Jr — a longtime vaccine skeptic — is being run by Danish researchers. Critics argue it treats African infants as test subjects for an agenda driven by vaccine doubt rather than scientific necessity.

What This Means: This is what happens when health policy is set by ideology: vulnerable populations bear the risk. The trial resurrects troubling echoes of historical medical exploitation in Africa, and its approval signals how far vaccine skepticism has embedded itself into formal US health infrastructure.

India's "Cockroach" Party Leaders Are Free — For Now

Indian authorities released leaders of the Cockroach Janta Party, a youth movement, after detaining them Saturday before they could lead protests over voting rights in New Delhi. Small demonstrations continued despite the crackdown. The group's provocative name is a direct jab at the political establishment — they're leaning into being called pests by those in power.

What This Means: Pre-emptive detention of protest leaders before rallies even begin is a classic authoritarian playbook move — and it's increasingly showing up in the world's largest democracy. Youth-led voting rights movements gaining enough traction to warrant police intervention suggests India's political establishment is more nervous about the next generation than it's letting on.

🇫🇷 Français · Le point du jour

Glucksmann désigné candidat de la gauche sociale-démocrate : une victoire éclatante qui n'efface pas les fractures

Raphaël Glucksmann a été investi dès le premier tour de la primaire de la gauche sociale-démocrate, dimanche 11 octobre, avec 66 % des suffrages exprimés. L'eurodéputé de 46 ans, fondateur de Place Publique, écrase une concurrence qui ne parvient pas à se hisser à sa hauteur : le premier secrétaire du PS, Olivier Faure, plafonne à 14 %, tandis que Ségolène Royal, Jérôme Guedj et Emmanuel Maurel restent chacun sous la barre des 10 %. Fort de ce plébiscite, Glucksmann a immédiatement promis de « rassembler la gauche démocratique » afin de « battre l'extrême droite » en 2027.

Ce scrutin interne s'inscrit dans un contexte de recomposition profonde de la gauche française depuis la dissolution de l'Assemblée nationale en 2024. La coalition du Nouveau Front Populaire, qui avait momentanément réuni socialistes, écologistes, communistes et La France Insoumise, a depuis volé en éclats sur les questions programmatiques et stratégiques. Glucksmann s'est imposé comme la figure de proue d'une gauche résolument social-démocrate, pro-européenne et atlantiste, désireuse de se démarquer de la ligne radicale portée par Jean-Luc Mélenchon et ses successeurs à la tête de LFI.

La victoire du candidat est toutefois indissociable de la question des alliances, qui demeure le nœud gordien de cette gauche fragmentée. Glucksmann réaffirme son refus catégorique de tout accord avec LFI, une posture qui lui garantit une cohérence idéologique mais réduit mécaniquement son réservoir électoral au premier tour. Sans l'apport des quelque quatre millions d'électeurs mélenchonistes, la route vers le second tour de la présidentielle s'annonce étroite, dans un paysage dominé à droite par un Rassemblement national structurellement ancré dans les premiers sondages.

Il y a là une ironie politique savoureuse : c'est en rejetant toute union de la gauche au sens large que Glucksmann entend incarner le rassemblement. L'homme qui a bâti sa notoriété sur la dénonciation des compromissions morales et des impasses idéologiques se retrouve lui-même prisonnier d'un dilemme arithmétique que les primaires ne peuvent pas résoudre. La gauche sociale-démocrate a trouvé son champion ; elle n'a pas encore trouvé sa majorité.

目の見えない男性、酔っぱらって高速道路を241キロ走行！

イギリスに住む50歳さいの男性だんせいが、お酒さけを飲のんだ状態じょうたいで高速道路こうそくどうろを約241キロ（150マイルまいる）も運転うんてんしました。この男性だんせいは目めが見みえないだけでなく、運転免許証うんてんめんきょしょうも持もっていませんでした。

警察けいさつはこの信しんじられない出来事できごとに非常ひじょうに驚おどろきました。どうやってそれほど長ながい距離きょりを走はしることができたのか、今いまでもよくわかっていないそうです。この男性だんせいは無事ぶじに生いき残のこったものの、周まわりを走はしっていたほかのドライバーどらいばーたちはどれほど驚おどろいたでしょうか。

この男性だんせいは飲酒運転いんしゅうんてんと無免許運転むめんきょうんてんという二ふたつの重大じゅうだいな違反いはんを犯おかしました。さらに、視覚障害しかくしょうがいがあるにもかかわらず運転うんてんしたという点てんで、この事件じけんは世界中せかいじゅうのニュースで話題わだいになりました。専門家せんもんかたちは、これがいかに危険きけんなことだったかを強調きょうちょうしています。

That Nature Is a Heraclitean Fire and of the Comfort of the Resurrection

by Gerard Manley Hopkins

Cloud-puffball, torn tufts, tossed pillows ' flaunt forth, then chevy on an air-
built thoroughfare: heaven-roysterers, in gay-gangs ' they throng; they glitter in marches.
Down roughcast, down dazzling whitewash, ' wherever an elm arches,
Shivelights and shadowtackle in long ' lashes lace, lance, and pair.
Delightfully the bright wind boisterous ' ropes, wrestles, beats earth bare
Of yestertempest's creases; in pool and rut peel parches
Squandering ooze to squeezed ' dough, crust, dust; stanches, starches
Squadroned masks and manmarks ' treadmire toil there
Footfretted in it. Million-fuelèd, ' nature's bonfire burns on.
But quench her bonniest, dearest ' to her, her clearest-selvèd spark
Man, how fast his firedint, ' his mark on mind, is gone!
Both are in an unfathomable, all is in an enormous dark
Drowned. O pity and indig ' nation! Manshape, that shone
Sheer off, disseveral, a star, ' death blots black out; nor mark
Is any of him at all so stark
But vastness blurs and time ' beats level. Enough! the Resurrection,
A heart's-clarion! Away grief's gasping, ' joyless days, dejection.
Across my foundering deck shone
A beacon, an eternal beam. ' Flesh fade, and mortal trash
Fall to the residuary worm; ' world's wildfire, leave but ash:
In a flash, at a trumpet crash,
I am all at once what Christ is, ' since he was what I am, and
This Jack, joke, poor potsherd, ' patch, matchwood, immortal diamond,
Is immortal diamond.
